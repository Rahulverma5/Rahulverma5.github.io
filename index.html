Knowledge-aware news image captioning

Writes the caption a news editor would, naming the people and places in the photo. Named entities from the article become knowledge prompts for FLAN-T5; CLIP and BLIP describe what the image shows; GPT-2 writes the caption from the combined multimodal context.
